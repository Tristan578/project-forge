Add a music track/clip arrangement data model and a manual arrangement editor. Generated and imported audio now share one editable timeline: create tracks, place clips, trim their in/out points, move them, and toggle looping from the new Music Arrangement panel. The arrangement is saved with the project alongside the scene, so it survives closing and reopening. Generating music now places the result on the timeline as a clip instead of a one-shot, play-only result.
