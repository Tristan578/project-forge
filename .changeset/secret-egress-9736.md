Stop upstream provider text reaching clients, and enforce it (#9736). The generation clients fold the upstream **response body** verbatim into the thrown error (`Meshy status error (401): <body>`), and twelve routes returned that error's `message` in their response — so on the platform path, where the credential in play is the platform's rather than the user's, a provider that echoes key material in an auth-failure body would hand a platform secret to any signed-in user. Those routes now return fixed text and send the detail to Sentry instead; `/api/game/decompose` keeps returning its safety-filter reason, but narrowed by a new typed `PromptRejectedError` rather than by a message prefix any upstream error could also produce. Two enforcement layers back it: `src/app/api/__tests__/noRawErrorEgress.test.ts` walks every route, follows the caught error through local assignments and balanced multi-line response calls, and fails when a response carries text derived from a caught error; and `redactSecrets()` runs inside both response constructors and inside the Sentry string scrubber (covering the event, log and metric pipelines at once), removing known credential shapes plus, by exact match, the values of every secret-named environment variable — the half that also covers a provider added later whose key format nobody has seen.
