Grant every brand-new account a one-time allocation of 50 trial tokens (`TRIAL_GRANT_TOKENS`) on the Clerk `user.created` webhook, and let a free account spend them: while a `starter` account holds spendable tokens every AI gate treats it as `hobbyist`, so chat and the hobbyist generation panels open until the balance is gone. The gates are `/api/chat` and `/api/game/decompose` (`assertAiAccess`), the platform-key resolver, the editor's panel gate, and a new server-side panel gate: every `/api/generate/*` route declares the panel it fronts, and the factory and `voice/batch` refuse a caller whose panel would render locked (`panelTierGateResponse`), so a trial account never reaches a creator-tier generator. Status pollers use `panelTierGateResponseForPoll` instead, which ignores the balance. A `starter` counts as `hobbyist` whatever it holds, so creator-tier status routes stay refused, while a trial user whose one generation spent the whole grant still receives the result. The resolver skips its tier and balance checks for the matching zero-cost `STATUS_CHECK_OPERATION` call (the pollers and the QStash `generation-complete` callback), because the job was paid for at creation. That also ends a lockout already on `main`: a hobbyist whose last generation left the balance at exactly 0 could not poll it, and the durable callback refunded the job instead of delivering it. The generation poller now shows the status route's `message` sentence instead of a bare code such as `TIER_REQUIRED`. The free plan's bullets now say "50 trial AI tokens at signup" and "No monthly AI tokens". The grant is idempotent at the database (a `trial_grant` row in `credit_transactions` is the arbiter behind the existing partial unique index), never runs on `user.updated`, and a failure is captured in Sentry with `context: trial-token-grant-failure` before the webhook's existing retry path handles it. Adds the P1 alert rule and runbook section 5.9 (#7715).
