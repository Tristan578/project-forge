"Make me a game" now asks before it spends build tokens. After you describe your game, the dialog designs it and shows the plan with its estimated token cost; the build starts only when you press "Build it", and Cancel releases the tokens held for it. For a first-time user who picked "Build with AI" in the welcome wizard, onboarding now counts as complete only once that build finishes. If it fails, is cancelled, or the dialog is closed before anything is built, the welcome wizard comes back.
