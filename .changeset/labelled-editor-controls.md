Give every colour picker, slider and dropdown in the editor an accessible name. Scene Settings, the light and material inspectors, and the export, generation and physics panels now pair each control with its visible label, so assistive technology reads the label as the control's name and clicking a label focuses its control. Each material texture slot's Upload button now names the slot it fills. Scene Settings is back under the accessibility audit with no exemption.
