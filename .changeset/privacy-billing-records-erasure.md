The Privacy Policy no longer says payment records are kept for at least 7 years. Deleting your account already deletes your billing history, token purchases and credit transactions, and the policy now says so: they are kept while your account exists and deleted with it. Stripe, the payment processor, keeps its own records of your payments under its own privacy policy. The policy's "Last updated" date is now November 16, 2026.
