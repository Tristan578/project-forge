SpawnForge now refuses use and payment from sanctioned countries and regions (#9331). A request whose Vercel geolocation (`x-vercel-ip-country`, and `x-vercel-ip-country-region` for the Ukrainian regions) matches the policy list gets a plain 451 "not available in your region" page, or a 451 JSON error for API calls, before sign-in or any app code runs. The list is Cuba, Iran, North Korea, the Crimea, Sevastopol, Donetsk and Luhansk regions of Ukraine, Russia, Belarus, China, and every other country with a country-based program on OFAC's Sanctions Programs page (Afghanistan, Burma, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Hong Kong, Iraq, Lebanon, Libya, Mali, Nicaragua, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Syria, Venezuela, Yemen). The checkout, billing portal, token purchase and marketplace purchase routes also refuse those locations themselves. Health checks, the Stripe and Clerk webhooks, Vercel cron and the generation-complete callback are never blocked by country. A request with no country header is allowed. The list lives in `web/src/lib/security/sanctionsGeoBlock.ts` and the spec at `specs/2026-11-04-sanctions-geo-block.md`; it is a policy list, not legal advice.
