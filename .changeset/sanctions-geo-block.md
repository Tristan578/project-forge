SpawnForge no longer sells or licenses anything to a buyer in a sanctioned country or region (#9331); site content stays open to every country. Starting a Checkout (paid plan or token pack), buying a marketplace asset, and opening the billing portal's plan-switching home page answer 451 "Purchases are not available in your region." when the buyer's Vercel IP country (and region, for Ukraine) is on the policy list; the portal's cancel flow stays open so customers can always cancel. A buyer whose country is unknown is not refused: Checkout now always requires a billing address, and the Stripe webhook grants no plan, tokens or paid features to a listed billing address (or, for a token pack, a card issued in a listed country), refunds the payment and cancels the subscription. The list is Cuba, Iran, North Korea, the Crimea, Sevastopol, Donetsk and Luhansk regions of Ukraine, the Zaporizhzhia and Kherson regions of Ukraine (an EU measure the owner follows, not an OFAC one), Russia, Belarus, China, and every other country with a country-based program on OFAC's Sanctions Programs page (Afghanistan, Burma, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Hong Kong, Iraq, Lebanon, Libya, Mali, Nicaragua, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Syria, Venezuela, Yemen). The proxy matcher also now reaches dynamic pages whose last segment looks like a file name (`/editor/abc.html`), so they get the auth redirect and header stripping, and skips Vercel's own `/_vercel/*` endpoints. The list lives in `web/src/lib/security/sanctionsGeoBlock.ts` and the spec at `specs/2026-11-04-sanctions-geo-block.md`; it is a policy list, not legal advice.
